CG privacy

CG is a personal program that runs on my own computer. I am its only user. This page says what it does with data from Google.

What it reads: with my permission, CG reads the events on my Google calendars (titles, times, places). It asks for read-only access and cannot create, change or delete anything.

Where it goes: the events are kept on my computer so the calendar can draw them. They are not sent to any server, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising or to train anything.

How long: the copy on my computer is replaced each time the calendar refreshes. Removing CG's access in my Google account settings stops it reading, and the local copy can be deleted at any time.
